How this works · PWAR, RWAR, the two dials, trade fairness

PWAR = expected playoff matchup wins a player adds over a waiver replacement. RWAR = the same across the entire regular season, week by week on the real schedule (playoff weeks excluded). A matchup = winning the majority of your league's categories (5 of 9 standard, set yours below), so stacking a category you already win adds almost nothing, and fixing a coin-flip category adds a lot. Playoff output runs on two separate dials: Games, will he even play (health, team rest policy, back-to-backs, travel), and Effort, how hard he plays the games he's in (contract incentives, team stakes). Output = games × production × effort. Trade suggestions are held to market value (ADP): we only propose swaps another manager would plausibly accept. Pickups are free, so there only playoff impact matters. All numbers are projections for entertainment only, not professional, legal, or financial advice.
