Copy your league in

You bring your own roster, we never ask for your password. It takes about a minute.

Don't worry about being neat. Select loosely and paste, even a whole roster table with stats, positions, and "Last, First" names. PlayoffProof reads the text and picks out the players automatically. When in doubt, grab more than you need.
You never have to figure out other teams. To see free agents, you do not reconstruct everyone else's roster. Your platform already has an Available Players (Yahoo) / Add Players (ESPN) page that shows exactly who's unrostered, it does the math for you. You just copy that list. And you only need the top of it, sorted by rank, since PlayoffProof only ever recommends good pickups, not the 200th-best free agent.

Sleeper easiest, no copying

  1. On the connect screen, type your Sleeper username and tap Connect.
  2. That's it, we pull your roster and everyone else's automatically, so Available is exact. If you're in more than one league, you'll pick which.

Yahoo copy + paste

Easiest on a computer (or your phone's browser in "desktop site" mode), because you're selecting text from a table.

  1. Open Yahoo Fantasy and go to your My Team page. This is your roster.
  2. Drag to select the list of your players (names are all that matters), then copy: Cmd+C on Mac, Ctrl+C on Windows.
  3. Paste it into the Your roster box in PlayoffProof.

To make Available exact (optional but worth it, it's the waiver edge):

  1. Click the Players tab, then set the filter to Available. This is your free-agent list, already computed for you, no need to check other teams.
  2. Sort by rank, select the top chunk (the first screen is plenty), copy, and paste into the Available players box in PlayoffProof.

ESPN copy + paste

Same idea. Do it on a computer if you can, it's much easier to select a table there than in the mobile app.

  1. Open ESPN Fantasy and go to your team's Roster page.
  2. Select your players and copy (Cmd/Ctrl + C).
  3. Paste into the Your roster box in PlayoffProof.

For exact Available:

  1. Go to Add Players, ESPN's free-agent list, already filtered to who's unrostered.
  2. Leave it sorted by rank, select the top chunk, copy, and paste into the Available players box.
Phone only? The team pages are hard to select in the mobile apps. Open your league in your phone's web browser and switch on "Request Desktop Site," or just type your ~12 starters into the Your roster box by hand, that works too.
