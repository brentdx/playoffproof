Refund Policy

Effective August 20, 2026

We want PlayoffProof to be worth it. If it is not, here is how refunds work.

14-day money-back guarantee

If you purchase a paid season pass and are not satisfied, email us within 14 days of your purchase and we will refund you in full, no questions asked. Refunds are issued to your original payment method through Stripe and typically appear within 5 to 10 business days.

After 14 days

Because a season pass grants access to time-sensitive, season-long analytics, purchases are generally non-refundable after the 14-day window. If something goes wrong on our end, for example an extended outage or a billing error, contact us and we will make it right, which may include a prorated or full refund at our discretion.

Duplicate or accidental charges

If you were charged more than once or billed in error, contact us and we will refund the incorrect charge promptly.

One account per league

Paid access may be limited to one manager per fantasy league. If your purchase could not be activated because another manager in your league already claimed access, contact us and we will refund you in full.

How to request a refund

Email support@playoffproof.com from the address associated with your purchase, with your receipt or the email you used at checkout. We aim to respond within 2 business days.
