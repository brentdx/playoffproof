The only time money is returned

We return your payment only when you could not receive the product in the first place:

  • Your league was already claimed. We admit one manager per league. If another manager in your league claimed PlayoffProof before you, your purchase cannot be activated and you are refunded 100% automatically, usually within minutes.

Neither of these is a satisfaction or change-of-mind refund; they simply return money for a product you were never able to use.
